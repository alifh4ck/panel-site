Refund & dispute policy

Clear terms, so there are no surprises.

Wrong or duplicate payment
If you send the wrong amount, or pay twice by mistake, contact support with your Order ID and TxID — refunds for these cases are reviewed and processed once verified.
Payment sent but not delivered
If your TxID is confirmed on-chain but the panel wasn't delivered, message support — this is resolved by delivery or refund, whichever you prefer.
After the panel is activated
Once a panel has been delivered and activated on your account, it can't be refunded for change of mind.
Sent to the wrong wallet or network
Crypto transactions can't be reversed once confirmed. Always double-check the wallet address and network shown at checkout before sending.
Disputes: open one via the order message thread above or contact support directly with your Order ID and TxID — disputes are reviewed within 12–24 hours.

FAQ

Common questions about paying safely.

Is it safe to pay in USDT before I get the panel?
Every order is checked manually against the transaction on-chain before it's marked verified. Always confirm the wallet address and network shown in the checkout modal — never send funds to an address from anywhere else.
What if I sent the wrong amount?
Submit your TxID as normal, then message support with your Order ID. Underpayments and overpayments are reviewed case by case — see the refund policy above.
How long does verification take?
Most orders are checked once the transaction has enough network confirmations. You can track live status anytime under "Track your order" using your Order ID and email.
Can I get a refund after the panel is delivered?
No — once a panel is activated it can't be refunded for change of mind. If something's wrong with what was delivered, contact support and it'll be looked into.
How do I contact support?
Use the message thread on your order, the contact section below, or the help button in the corner of the page — email is monitored, and Telegram if you gave a username at checkout.
